HeXie uses texgyreheros-bold.otf and texgyreschola-bold.otf from the TeX Gyre distribution.
Both font binaries are unmodified. A complete unmodified upstream distribution
is included as tex-gyre-original.zip, with its manifests, licenses and sources.
Upstream: https://ctan.org/pkg/tex-gyre
Download: https://mirrors.ctan.org/fonts/tex-gyre.zip
